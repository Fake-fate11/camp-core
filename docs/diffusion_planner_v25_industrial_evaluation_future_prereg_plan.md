# V25 Future Industrial-Oriented Endpoint and Preregistration Plan

This document freezes a future-plan boundary. It authorizes no model, pool,
selector, calibration, validation, closed-loop, Fresh, holdout, training, or
claim action.

## 1. Required pre-outcome contract

Before any future same-ego batch8 experiment, freeze:

1. generator, latent, model, checkpoint, input-state, forward, pool, tensor,
   candidate0=row0, and post-pool zero-call bindings;
2. exact endpoint registry version and capability matrix;
3. scenario, route, geometry, source, seed, and latent manifests;
4. cluster assignment and equal-cluster weighting;
5. planned pair, arm, run, tick, opportunity, and failure denominators;
6. hard safety, guardrail/NI, and multiplicity topology;
7. numeric margins only where an external or independent prospective basis
   exists;
8. typed missing and retained-failure rules;
9. target-architecture latency instrumentation;
10. an independent reviewer that does not import producer metric or decision
    oracles.

## 2. Primary endpoint families

### Hard-safety candidates

Prospective hard-safety topology may include:

- full-polygon collision any/episodes/duration;
- certified unthresholded red crossing;
- full-footprint road containment;
- dynamic critical exposure endpoints.

No numeric margin is presently authorized. Until future preregistration, each
machine field remains
`numeric_margin_not_authorized_until_future_preregistration`.

Collision severity, THW, PET, and unique-direction wrong-way must fail closed
when their semantic authority is missing.

### Operations guardrails

Prospective guardrails may include stateful route progress/completion, goal
status, backtracking, speed excess, traveled distance, travel efficiency, and
false-stop endpoints. False-stop cannot enter a gate until motion-opportunity
and exclusion contexts are instrumented and its speed/minimum-duration rules
are preregistered.

### Planar kinematic proxy

Longitudinal/lateral filtered acceleration, descriptive planar VDV-like, and
filtered-jerk control smoothness may be reported as vehicle-body planar
kinematic proxies. They cannot be labeled occupant/seat comfort, ISO 2631
conformity, SAE J2834 conformity, or an industrial comfort PASS.

### Realtime

Instrument pool generation, atoms, context/weights, selector increment, and
end-to-end timing separately on the target same-ego batch8 architecture.
Warm-up, concurrency, load, and deadline scheduler evidence must be added before
any production realtime statement.

## 3. Required new nonholdout instrumentation

Minimum additions are:

- contact-time body velocity/contact dynamics if collision severity is desired;
- unique lane-leader identity for THW;
- frozen conflict zones and passage times for PET;
- false-stop opportunity plus red/obstacle/goal-wait exclusion receipts;
- unambiguous lane/route direction receipts;
- target batch8 per-stage timing and runtime-condition receipts.

Occupant/seat comfort requires a separate evidence program with seat,
suspension, human-transfer, vertical, rotational, frequency-weighted, and
qualified measurement channels. It is scientifically inapplicable to the
current simulator evidence and cannot be repaired with a planar proxy.

## 4. Statistical topology

1. Summarize every endpoint per run.
2. Keep missing/failure members in the complete planned denominator.
3. Aggregate inside each preregistered scenario/corridor-intersection cluster.
4. Weight clusters equally.
5. Use a two-sided Student-t CI95 across cluster values unless a different
   method is prospectively justified and frozen.
6. Use endpoint direction to count paired better/tie/worse; exact zero delta is
   a tie.
7. Do not assign direction to signed means, constants, opportunities, or other
   descriptive-unclassified fields.
8. Freeze a familywise multiplicity method before outcomes.
9. Never create a weighted total.
10. Never replace full paired inference with a complete-case subset.

## 5. Decision matrix

| Condition | Future decision |
|---|---|
| Root/schema/finite/full-denominator integrity fails | Evidence failure; no scientific decision |
| Required hard-safety endpoint missing | Fail closed for the relevant claim family |
| Numeric margin lacks prospective authority | No NI/guardrail decision for that endpoint |
| All preregistered vector gates pass | Only the exact preregistered bounded claim may be considered |
| Any endpoint fails | Preserve vector result; no weighted compensation |
| Seat/vertical/transfer evidence missing | Occupant comfort remains not assessed |
| Target latency runtime conditions missing | Production realtime remains not assessed |

## 6. Relationship to current training-support work

This endpoint plan does not automatically restore the superseded
training-support reference or batch8 calibration. Control must decide whether
that work is still useful for selector compatibility, whether an
adaptation/retraining decision needs separate evidence, or whether a future
batch8 calibration should be redesigned around this evaluation system.

No SafetyCost, legacy 14D score distribution, or calibration repeatability
result may become a primary industrial endpoint or retrospective claim gate.
